# Yellowbet.com Landing Page — Current State Assessment

## Date: April 2026

---

## 1. Current Page Structure

The existing yellowbet.com is a **minimal country-selector page** consisting of:

- **Header**: "YOUR ULTIMATE BETTING DESTINATION" (plain text, no supporting imagery)
- **Country Grid**: 10 clickable flag icons linking to local brand sites
- **Footer**: "© All Rights Reserved"
- **No other content** — no value proposition, no offers, no sports context

## 3. Click-Through & Conversion Analysis

### Why Kenya Converts Well
- **Brand consistency**: TV ad says "Yellowbet" → landing page → yellowbet.ke — seamless brand journey
- **Language match**: English EPL/UCL audience → English landing page → English site
- **New market excitement**: Launched Dec 2025, fresh brand awareness, likely strong welcome offers

### Why Other Markets Convert Poorly

#### Critical Issue: **Brand Disconnect**
Users in Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia see a **Yellowbet** TV ad during EPL/UCL, arrive at yellowbet.com, then are redirected to **GSB** (Gal Sports Betting) — a completely different brand name. This creates:
- **Trust erosion**: "I clicked on Yellowbet but ended up somewhere else"
- **Confusion**: Users may think they're on the wrong site
- **Abandonment**: Users bounce rather than register on an unfamiliar brand

The same issue affects:
- **Liberia** → Starbet (different brand)
- **Mozambique/Rwanda** → Winner (different brand)

#### Secondary Issues
1. **No value proposition**: The page offers zero reason to click. No welcome bonus, no odds preview, no match highlights
2. **No geo-detection**: Users must manually find their country among 10 flags — friction for mobile users (majority in Africa)
3. **No brand explanation**: Nothing explains the relationship between Yellowbet and GSB/Winner/Starbet
4. **No sports context**: TV ad runs during EPL/UCL but the landing page has zero football content
5. **No urgency**: No live match data, no time-sensitive offers, no "bet now" motivation
6. **Poor mobile experience**: Small flag grid on mobile screens, easy to mis-tap
7. **No social proof**: No user count, no testimonials, no trust signals
8. **No localization cues**: Page doesn't speak the user's language or reference local context
9. **Slow cognitive load**: User must process 10 countries, identify theirs, then decide to click
10. **No fallback**: Users from non-listed countries (other African markets, diaspora) have nowhere to go

## 4. Funnel Drop-Off Points

```
TV Ad (EPL/UCL on DSTV/Canal+)
    ↓ ✅ Good awareness, high intent
yellowbet.com arrives
    ↓ ❌ No immediate value shown — "is this legit?"
User scans for their country
    ↓ ❌ Small flags, manual search, friction
User clicks flag
    ↓ ❌ Lands on GSB/Winner/Starbet — "this isn't Yellowbet"
User bounces
```

## 5. Competitive Benchmark

Leading African betting brands (Betway Africa, Sportybet, 1xBet) use:
- Geo-detection with auto-redirect
- Prominent welcome bonuses on landing pages
- Live match previews and odds
- Strong mobile-first design
- Single consistent brand across markets
